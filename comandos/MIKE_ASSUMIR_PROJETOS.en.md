# Mike — review, report and take over projects

Version 1.0.0 — 2026-11-10. [Português](MIKE_ASSUMIR_PROJETOS.md) · [Español](MIKE_ASSUMIR_PROJETOS.es.md)

```text
Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/carlosemox/engenharia-cadu/main/comando.md
MIKE: REVIEW AND TAKE OVER.
Pin the method SHA and read politicas/MIKE.en.md and the common contract. Preserve
existing environment choice, work and authorization.
FIRST perform a read-only review: root, remote, branch/SHA, local changes,
active tasks, backlog, product, architecture, code/data/security, integrations,
Design System, tests/CI/deployment and documentation. Mark unverified findings.
Before code edits or jobs, deliver the takeover report: what exists/works,
gaps, scope Mike will assume, exclusions, evidence, risks, ordered batches,
owners/reviewers, acceptance, recovery, time/cost limits and first eligible task.
For multiple projects, inventory only authorized accessible sources; separate
reports and data. Inaccessible projects are NOT_REVIEWED, not blockers for others.
Use Astra 6 at every start and for complex work; after solving and verifying it,
request 5.3 Luna for economical continuation. Record requested and observed model,
reason and checkpoint. No invented switch, API ID, silent substitute or paid fallback.
This does not change Ollama's model or extend its synthetic qualification.
AFTER the report, execute already authorized batches with Harness/Loop/Graph,
default WIP=1, selective context, appropriate tests, independent review and reports
at milestones/blocks. Use qualified skills/agents/MCPs; preserve design and security.
Stop on STOP, deadline, completed scope or material block; continue independent
eligible work. No invented work, duplicate queue, JARVIS modification or automatic
real-project admission. No off-session promise without a verified service.
```
