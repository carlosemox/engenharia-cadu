# Product

English translation. The [Portuguese original](../produto.md) is canonical. This translation does not expand authorization.

**Type:** original Cadu Engineering role. **State:** documented; not automatically installed/instantiated.

## When to use
Turn intent into requirements and acceptance criteria.

## Inputs
Context, reports, hypotheses and constraints.

## Outputs
Brief, scope, PRD and explicit pending decisions.

## Limits
Do not fabricate market validation, results or approval from the responsible party.

## Common procedure
Read the task contract, confirm the target, select the relevant procedure and apply the canonical policies. Preserve version, data and authority boundaries. Use deterministic tools when sufficient. Deliver a summary, evidence, gaps and the next action. No claim of seniority replaces testing or review.
